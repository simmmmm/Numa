Numa's own camera profiles, v1

Numa's own work. Each profile was fitted by Numa (tests/camera_profile_fit.rs,
RENDER-012/015) from raw files that raw.pixls.us publishes under CC0 1.0
(public domain), against the JPEG each camera embedded in its own file. No
other camera profile, and no photograph under any other licence, went into
them. They are offered as options beside a camera's matrix and any other
profile; Numa never chooses one of them by itself.

Each is shipped only because it beat the camera's matrix on held-out frames
(frames the fit never saw), as mean chroma error against the camera's JPEG —
refitted on 27 September on the cameras' tone curve (RENDER-004), on which the
matrix alone is already nearer the JPEGs than it was. The E-M1 Mark II's is
gone: fitted then against Numa's own rendering, since rawler reads no preview
from an ORF, and fitted again against the camera's it did not beat the matrix.

The X-T5 profile is the one exception to the raw.pixls.us rule: it was fitted
from ten of the photographer's own X-T5 frames (Provia, Color 0) against the
JPEG each carries, three of them held out, on the cameras' tone curve
(RENDER-004, 27 September). Against the camera's HEIF of the same frames the
median colour difference (CIEDE2000) goes from 1.86 to 1.07, where Capture One's
Provia is 1.06 and Lightroom's Adobe Color 1.47.

The raws: https://raw.pixls.us/getfile.php/<number>/nice/ (CC0 1.0,
